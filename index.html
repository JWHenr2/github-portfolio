About Me

My career has taken an unconventional path into game development. I spent over 20 years in the United States Marine Corps working in technical, cybersecurity, training, and leadership roles, including serving as a Marine Corps Drill Instructor. Throughout that career, I led and developed personnel, communicated complex technical concepts, evaluated performance, coordinated technical teams, and helped people succeed in demanding environments.

After retiring from the Marine Corps, I had the opportunity to pursue a goal I had wanted to follow since high school: game development. I completed my Bachelor of Science in Game Development at Full Sail University and am continuing my education through a Master of Science in Game Design.

That experience has given me an interesting perspective on technical education. I have spent much of my career developing and mentoring others, and I have also recently experienced what it is like to be the student learning modern technologies such as Unreal Engine 5, C++, Blueprint, Unity, source control, and collaborative development workflows.

I am particularly interested in the intersection of technology, game development, leadership, and education, and in helping people learn the tools and skills that allow them to turn ideas into interactive experiences.
