About Me

Before moving into game development, I spent more than 20 years in the United States Marine Corps working in cybersecurity and technical leadership roles. That career taught me how to solve difficult problems, work as part of a team, lead people, and keep learning when the technology or situation changes.

Game development is something I had wanted to pursue since graduating high school, but it wasn't something I was able to do at the time. After retiring from the Marine Corps, I finally had the opportunity to come back to that goal and enrolled in the Game Development program at Full Sail University. Since then, I've worked with Unreal Engine 5, Unity, C++, C#, Blueprints, gameplay systems, UI, audio, and other areas of game development.

What I've enjoyed most is actually seeing something I've helped build come to life on the screen. I'm continuing to grow as a developer, with a particular interest in Unreal Engine, C++, gameplay programming, and creating systems that make games more interactive and engaging for the player.
